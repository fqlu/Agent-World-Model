Every scope folder has an `_Overview.md` (context: what it is, active
workstreams, key people, recent activity, reference) and may have a
`_Tasks.md` (open commitments). Folders named `… | WIP Notes` hold dated
meeting/work notes (`YYYY-MM-DD.md`).

## Operating rules

1. **Read before write.** Entering a scope: `_Overview.md` → `_Tasks.md` (if
   present) → recent WIP notes as needed. Don't act on a scope you haven't read.
2. **Retrieve by address.** Context for client X's project Y is at
   `Clients/X/X | Y/_Overview.md` — go there; don't trawl.
3. **Merge, don't overwrite.** Same-date notes and journal files are merged
   into. Edit overviews surgically; never regenerate them wholesale.
4. **Close the loop.** After changing files: add a line to the scope's
   `## Recent activity` (newest first, keep ≤10) and to today's journal under
   `## Agent activity` (`- HH:MM · <agent> · <scope> — what → [link]`).
5. **Never delete.** Move superseded material to `Archive/` mirroring its
   path, and journal the move.
6. **Tasks have a home.** Open commitments live in the scope's `_Tasks.md`.
   Completed items move to `## Done` — they are memory, not clutter.
7. **Naming is load-bearing.** Scope folders carry their ancestry:
   `<Entity short> | <Unit> | <Sub-project>`. Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
   Renames cascade to descendants — propose, don't improvise.
8. **Propose structural changes.** New scopes, renames, moves, migrations:
   present a plan to the human before executing. Content edits within the
   rituals above don't need approval.
9. **Ask when ambiguous.** A wrong-but-confident filing costs more than a
   question.

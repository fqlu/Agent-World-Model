Key: **areas** hold scopes (`<Area>/<Entity>/<Entity short> | <Unit>/…`);
**journal** holds `YYYY/YYYY-MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md` (daily index + agent log);
**templates** holds canonical templates (always copy, never invent layouts);
**archive** holds superseded material, mirroring original paths;
**context** (if mounted): ANY folder may contain a context directory of that
name (default `_Context/`) holding one markdown file per group (frontmatter:
name/title/members). Members are descendant folders of the declaring folder,
referenced by relative path. Membership INHERITS DOWNWARD — a folder's
effective context is its own memberships plus every ancestor's. Declare
groups at the lowest common ancestor of their members. For association
questions ("which X belong to Y?"), read the relevant context directories
FIRST: they are the authoritative source of declared relationships.
Expanding a group: each member folder plus all its descendants.

**Folders are content by default.** A folder with an `_Overview.md` is a
**scope** (a maintained unit of work — read its overview before acting
there); every top-level folder inside an area is a scope. A folder without
one is a **content folder** (documents, assets): it needs no context files —
its context is the nearest scope above it plus any context groups it belongs
to. Never add context machinery to content folders uninvited. Scopes may
also have a `_Tasks.md` (open commitments). Folders named `… | WIP Notes`
hold dated meeting/work notes (`YYYY-MM-DD.md`). A generated `_Index.md` at
the root maps every scope, entity, and context group — use it for
orientation, but the tree and the source files are canonical.

**Entities** (if an entities mount like `_Entities/` is declared) are the
"cast": people, systems, and concepts as addressable nodes, one file each.
When a name or system recurs (a colleague, a GTM container, a topic), the
entity file is its canonical record — resolve references there rather than
re-deriving from prose. Each carries provenance (`source`, `confirmed`).

A **reflection scaffold** may be handed to you after a sync — a list of files
that changed in the workspace. When you receive one, read those files and
propose world-model updates (entities, relationships, overview activity,
state) grounded in them; don't invent facts the files don't support.

**Relationships** are typed edges in an entity's `relations:` frontmatter
(`predicate`, `target`, `note`) — the verbs of the world model (leads, owns,
uses, deployed-for, depends-on…). To answer "who/what relates to X", read
the entities, or load the compiled **`_Model.json`** at the root: it is the
whole world model as nodes + edges in one queryable document — the fastest
way to traverse relationships without walking every file. `_Model.json` is
generated; the entity files are canonical.

## Operating rules

1. **Read before write.** Entering a scope: `_Overview.md` → `_Tasks.md` (if
   present) → recent WIP notes as needed. Don't act on a scope you haven't read.
2. **Retrieve by address.** Context for entity X's project Y is at
   `<Area>/X/X | Y/_Overview.md` — go there; don't trawl.
3. **Merge, don't overwrite.** Same-date notes and journal files are merged
   into. Edit overviews surgically; never regenerate them wholesale.
4. **Close the loop.** After changing files: add a line to the scope's
   `## Recent activity` (newest first, keep ≤10) and to today's journal under
   `## Agent activity` (`- HH:MM · <agent> · <scope> — what → [link]`).
5. **Never delete.** Move superseded material to `Archive/` mirroring its
   path, and journal the move.
6. **Tasks have a home.** Open commitments live in the scope's `_Tasks.md`.
   Completed items move to `## Done` — they are memory, not clutter.
7. **Naming is load-bearing.** Scope folders carry their ancestry:
   `<Entity short> | <Unit> | <Sub-project>`. Dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.
   Renames cascade to descendants — propose, don't improvise.
8. **Propose structural changes.** New scopes, renames, moves, migrations:
   present a plan to the human before executing. Content edits within the
   rituals above don't need approval.
9. **Ask when ambiguous.** A wrong-but-confident filing costs more than a
   question.
